# Human playtest kit

Bots can tell us whether the game works. Only people can tell us whether it's fun, whether the music fits and whether their own suburb feels like home. Aim for 3–5 people per phase.

## What you send them
1. The Vercel link (the phase's preview link, or the live site).
2. This script (about 10 minutes):
   - Open the link on whatever you normally use (phone, tablet or computer). Don't read any instructions first.
   - Play **Training night**.
   - Type a place you know well (your street is fine; it never leaves your browser) and play one mission it suggests.
   - Play one more mission, any kind.
3. The five questions below. Answers by text or voice note are fine.

## The five questions
1. In one sentence, what is this game?
2. When were you confused, and when were you bored?
3. What was the best moment?
4. Did your own area feel like your area? What was missing or wrong?
5. Would you play again tomorrow? Why or why not?

## What happens to the answers
The owner pastes them into a new GitHub issue labelled `playtest-human`, **removing any addresses or place names first**. The Experience Playtester turns them into backlog items, and human notes outrank bot statistics (see `docs/CREW.md`).
