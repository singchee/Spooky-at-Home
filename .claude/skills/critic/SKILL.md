# /critic — how would a reviewer score it?

1. Launch the `critic` subagent, ideally after a `/player-test` on the same build.
2. Show the owner the headline, the overall score, the score box, and "what would raise this by a full point".
3. Track the score over time in the Brief. **Goodhart warning:** the score is a lens, not a target. Don't chase points with changes that wouldn't make the game better for a real player.
